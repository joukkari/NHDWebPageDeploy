Description

Drunken Hog is a story-driven pub management game focused on brewing, running a bar, and narrative depth. It is set in a small open city where you are free to explore, meet locals, and uncover the story at your own pace.

You are now the proud new owner of Drunken Hog. Brew your own beer, serve the locals, keep the bar running, and let the choices you make shape the story around you.

Customers have their own lives, moods, and preferences. As your reputation grows, you can upgrade your equipment, expand the pub, and dig deeper into the strange, unsettling truths hiding in Napolis City.

History

Noir Hound Digital currently consists of two longtime friends who decided to start a game studio together. Drunken Hog is the studio's currently announced title and the public face of the project so far.

The tone of the studio and the game both lean into a noir-flavored mix of atmosphere, systems-driven play, and stories that feel slightly off in the best way.
